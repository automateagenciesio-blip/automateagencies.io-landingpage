CORE RULES - Use only the information provided. - Do not invent fake reviews, fake ratings, fake awards, fake years of experience, fake history, fake team members, fake statistics, or unsupported claims. - If some information is missing, simplify gracefully instead of making things up. - Keep the copy concise, natural, specific, and believable. - Do not create a generic AI-looking website. - Do not use filler content. - Do not bloat the page with unnecessary sections. - Prioritize clarity, trust, and conversion. DESIGN DIRECTION - Create a clean, premium, high-conversion layout. - Make the hero section strong and immediately useful. - Use clean spacing, clear hierarchy, and easy-to-scan sections. - Keep the website visually polished but realistic. - Match the style to the business type and brand style. - Prioritize mobile-first design. - Avoid startup-style jargon unless it genuinely fits the business. - Avoid clutter, overdesign, and flashy effects. COPY DIRECTION - Write like a real business website, not a marketing template. - Keep headlines clear and direct. - Keep body copy short. - Make every sentence relevant to this exact business. - Sound professional, trustworthy, and local. - Avoid hype and vague generic statements. You are improving the EXISTING Google AI Studio website. - Do NOT rebuild the project from scratch. - Do NOT redesign the website. - Do NOT change the business details. - Keep the current component structure, styling system, and premium design direction. Your task is to audit and fix every interactive element so the website works like a real client-ready website. This is a surgical pass: if something already works correctly, leave it exactly as it is. Only change what is broken, incomplete, or unclear. CHECK AND FIX Navbar links - Every navbar link smooth-scrolls to the correct section. - The sticky navbar must not cover section headings — use scroll-margin-top or an equivalent clean solution. - Mobile menu links work correctly and close the menu after navigating. Hero CTA buttons - The primary CTA performs the main business action; the secondary CTA performs the secondary action. - If a CTA should call the business, use a tel: link. If it should scroll to a section, connect it to the correct section. - No hero button is decorative only. Contact actions - Phone buttons use a valid tel: link. - Email buttons use a valid mailto: link. - WhatsApp buttons use a valid https://wa.me/ link, but ONLY if a WhatsApp number exists in configuration. - Directions buttons open the Google Maps link if available, or use the address as a fallback. - External links (WhatsApp, Google Maps) open in a new tab with target="_blank" and rel="noopener noreferrer". - Verify no link has an empty or placeholder href (e.g. "tel:", "https://wa.me/", "#"). An incomplete link counts as a broken link. Contact form - Keep the form frontend-only. Do not add a backend. - On submit, prevent the default action and show a simple, clearly worded frontend confirmation message. - The confirmation wording must not claim the message was sent to a server or that the business received it. Phrase it honestly (e.g. confirming the details were captured / asking the visitor to call or message for a faster response). - The submit button is clearly styled and labeled. General button quality - Every button, link, and CTA has a real purpose. Fix or remove dead buttons. - Button labels clearly describe the action. - Key contact actions stay visible in both the hero and the contact section. RULES - Do not invent business details, fake claims, fake reviews, fake ratings, or fake statistics. - Do not change the visual style unless a small adjustment is genuinely needed for clarity or usability. - Keep the website clean, premium, and consistent. BEFORE FINISHING - Trace the page flow from top to bottom: navigation, hero CTAs, contact links, and mobile menu behavior all make sense. - Then give a short, plain-language summary of what you changed and why.
