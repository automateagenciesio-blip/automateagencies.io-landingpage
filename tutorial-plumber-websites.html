CORE RULES - Use only the information provided. - Do not invent fake reviews, fake ratings, fake awards, fake years of experience, fake history, fake team members, fake statistics, or unsupported claims. - If some information is missing, simplify gracefully instead of making things up. - Keep the copy concise, natural, specific, and believable. - Do not create a generic AI-looking website. - Do not use filler content. - Do not bloat the page with unnecessary sections. - Prioritize clarity, trust, and conversion. DESIGN DIRECTION - Create a clean, premium, high-conversion layout. - Make the hero section strong and immediately useful. - Use clean spacing, clear hierarchy, and easy-to-scan sections. - Keep the website visually polished but realistic. - Match the style to the business type and brand style. - Prioritize mobile-first design. - Avoid startup-style jargon unless it genuinely fits the business. - Avoid clutter, overdesign, and flashy effects. COPY DIRECTION - Write like a real business website, not a marketing template. - Keep headlines clear and direct. - Keep body copy short. - Make every sentence relevant to this exact business. - Sound professional, trustworthy, and local. - Avoid hype and vague generic statements.
